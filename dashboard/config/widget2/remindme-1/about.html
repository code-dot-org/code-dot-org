About RemindMe

RemindMe helps users keep track of tasks and deadlines in one simple place. Add reminders, manage your list, and stay organized throughout the day.

Features

  • Create and manage reminders
  • Reorder tasks with a quick shuffle tool
  • Try different theme options
  • Access helpful guidance from the help menu

Our goal is to make task management feel simple, flexible, and easy to use.
