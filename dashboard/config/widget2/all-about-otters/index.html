Cool Otter Facts!

  • Did you know otters hold hands when they sleep so they don't drift apart? Awww!
  • They can also hold their breath for a loooong time underwater!

Otterly Amazing Swimmers!

Otters are super speedy in the water! They use their powerful tails and webbed feet to zoom around.

They can dive deep to find yummy snacks like fish and crabs.

Where in the World are Otters?

You can find different kinds of otters all over the globe, from chilly Alaska to sunny Brazil.

Some live in rivers, some in coasts, and some even in the open ocean!
