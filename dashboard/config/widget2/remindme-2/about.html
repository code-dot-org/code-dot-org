About RemindMe

RemindMe is a simple, student-friendly reminder app designed to demonstrate good UI patterns: clear labels, predictable actions, and helpful feedback.

What’s improved vs. Level 1?

  • Readable colors with strong contrast
  • Clear, labeled buttons with consistent behavior
  • Accessible structure (skip link, ARIA, focus states)
  • Theme toggle that reflects state and stores your preference
