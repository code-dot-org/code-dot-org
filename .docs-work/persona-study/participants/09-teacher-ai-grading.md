## Answering sentence
"AI evaluation for rubrics. When you grade a rubric-backed level, you can ask AI to suggest an evidence level for each learning goal. The AI reads the student's code and highlights relevant lines. You review, adjust, and submit the final scores."

On trust: "It does not assign final grades. A teacher always submits the rubric evaluation." and "The AI evaluation is a starting point; you review every suggestion and submit your own scores."

## Confusion points
- Button-name mismatch: review-ai-evaluation says "Run AI Assessment for Project" and "Run AI Assessment for Class." giving-feedback-and-grading says "Run AI Assessment." Three different names for what appears to be the same feature.
- "Confidence indicator" appears only on the grading page, never on the dedicated AI-evaluation page. The more detailed page is missing a detail the shorter page has.
- Neither page names which courses contain rubric-backed levels with AI evaluation. Both say "some levels" / "where available." Cannot answer "do I have it for my course" without opening a level.
- "You need to be a verified teacher with AI features enabled in your account" — no link or explanation for how to check "AI features enabled" or what to do if it is not.
