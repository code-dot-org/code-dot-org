# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@code.org. Do not open a public issue, discussion or pull
request for a vulnerability.

Include:

- the affected URL, or the file and line in this repository;
- steps to reproduce, and what an attacker gains;
- your name or handle, if you would like credit.

We read every report and reply to the address it came from.

## Scope

This repository and the sites it serves, including studio.code.org.

When testing, use accounts you created yourself. Do not access, change or
keep other people's data, in particular students' data; do not degrade the
service for others; and do not use social engineering or phishing against
Code.org staff, teachers or students.
