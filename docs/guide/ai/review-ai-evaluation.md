On rubric-backed levels with AI evaluation, you can ask CodeAI to analyze student code and suggest an evidence level for each learning goal. You review every suggestion and submit your own scores.

## Prerequisites

- You are a [verified teacher](/guide/professional-learning/become-a-verified-teacher/) with AI features enabled. To check, open any rubric level and look for the **Use AI features** toggle in the rubric settings panel.
- At least one student has submitted work on a rubric level.

## Request an AI evaluation

1. Open the student's work from the [progress grid](/guide/progress/tracking-progress/).
2. In the rubric panel, select **Run AI Assessment for Project**. To evaluate all students at once, select **Run AI Assessment for Class**.
3. A status indicator shows when the evaluation finishes.

Each student can receive up to 10 AI evaluations per project.

## Review the suggestions

For each learning goal, the AI shows:

- A suggested evidence level: **Extensive Evidence**, **Convincing Evidence**, **Limited Evidence**, or **No Evidence**.
- **Evidence:** specific code lines the AI considered, with a short explanation.

Read the evidence before accepting the score.

## Accept or override

Select a different evidence level on any learning goal to override the AI. When you are satisfied, select **Submit**. The student sees the evidence levels you submitted and any written feedback, not the AI's raw suggestions.

## Provide feedback on AI accuracy

After reviewing, you can answer the **Is AI accurate?** prompt for each learning goal. This feedback helps improve the model and does not change the score.

## Turn off AI evaluation

Open the rubric settings panel on any rubric level and turn off the **Use AI features** toggle under **AI Settings**. You can turn it back on at any time.
