You decide whether your students can use AI Chat on levels that offer it. When you assign a course that uses AI Chat, CodeAI turns access on by default.

## Prerequisites

- A section assigned to a course that includes AI Chat levels. The **AI Settings** tab appears only when this condition is met.
- You must be a [verified teacher](/guide/professional-learning/become-a-verified-teacher/). If you are not verified, the **AI Settings** tab shows a warning and a link to learn how.

## Change AI Chat access

1. On the teacher dashboard, select the section.
2. Select the **AI Settings** tab.

Under **Class Section Settings**, two controls determine access:

| Setting | What students can do |
|---|---|
| **AI Chat Tools** toggle on | Use AI Chat on any level that offers it. |
| Toggle off, **Allow essential AI features only** checked | Use AI Chat only on levels the course marks as essential. |
| Both off | No AI Chat access. Students cannot complete levels that require AI Chat tools. |

If the section's course requires AI Chat to complete and you disable both controls, an alert warns that students will not be able to finish some levels.

Students on an AI Chat level see a chat interface where they can type questions or prompts. They do not see the access-level setting.
