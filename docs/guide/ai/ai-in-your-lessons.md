Some courses include AI-powered tools. Your teacher decides whether your class has access to them.

**Applies to:** courses with AI features enabled.

## Use AI Chat in a lesson

Some levels include an AI Chat panel where you can ask questions about the lesson topic.

1. Open a level with AI Chat. The chat panel appears on the page.
2. Type your question in the text box and press **Enter** or select the send button.

The AI responds in the same panel. You can ask follow-up questions in the same conversation. The AI is scoped to the lesson topic; it does not answer questions outside that topic. If your input is considered inappropriate, an error appears instead of a response.

Your teacher has access to your AI Chat history for the class.

### If AI Chat is not available

Your teacher may have turned off AI Chat for your class.

## Train an AI model

In AI Lab levels, you train a small machine-learning model by giving it data, then test what it predicts. Training runs in your browser.

1. Open the AI Lab level.
2. Choose which data columns to use as inputs and which column the model should predict.
3. Select **Train**. A progress indicator shows how training is going.

After training, switch to the test view. Enter new values for the input columns to see what the model predicts. If the level allows it, select **Save** and give your model a name; saved models appear in your model list and can be loaded later.
