A free CodeAI account saves your progress and, for teachers, unlocks classroom tools such as sections, assignment, and progress tracking.

## Create a teacher account

1. Go to [studio.code.org/users/sign_up](https://studio.code.org/users/sign_up/account_type).
2. Select **I'm a teacher**.
3. Enter your email address, choose a password, and confirm it.
4. Select **Create my account**.
5. Enter your first name, last name, and display name.
6. Select your country. If you are in the United States, enter your zip code and select your school from the list.
7. Select your educator role and the grades you teach.
8. Select **Go to my account**.

CodeAI sends a verification email to the address you provided. Verify it so you can reset your password later.

## Create a student account

1. Go to [studio.code.org/users/sign_up](https://studio.code.org/users/sign_up/account_type).
2. Select **Student**.

   ![The account type chooser with a Student card and a Teacher card](images/create-an-account-account-type-chooser.png)

3. Enter your email address, choose a password, and confirm it.
4. Select **Sign up**.
5. Enter a display name and select your age. If you are in the United States, select your state.
6. Select **Go to my account**.

### About age and state

CodeAI uses age and state to follow children's privacy laws. In some US states, students under a certain age need a parent or guardian's permission before the account is fully active. See [Parent permission](/guide/privacy/parent-permission/).

### If a teacher already set up the account

The teacher may have given the student a section code and a picture or word password. See [Sign in](/guide/getting-started/sign-in/) instead.

## Sign up with Google, Microsoft, Clever, or ClassLink

Instead of entering an email and password, select **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Microsoft**, **Continue with Clever**, or **Continue with ClassLink** on the sign-up page. You finish account creation on the provider's site, then return to CodeAI.
