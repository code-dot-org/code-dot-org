You can switch between student and teacher account types from **Settings**.

## Switch from student to teacher

1. Select your name in the top-right corner, then select **Settings**.
2. Under **Account Type**, change the dropdown to **Teacher** and select **Update Account Type**.
3. Enter your email address in the confirmation dialog and select **Update to teacher account**.

Your existing progress and projects stay with your account.

## Switch from teacher to student

Switching from teacher to student is permanent and removes access to all teacher features.

### Prerequisites

- You do not own any active sections. If you own sections, delete or transfer them first. Teachers with only demo sections (created automatically, with no real students) can switch without removing them.

### Steps

1. Select your name in the top-right corner, then select **Settings**.
2. Under account type, select **Switch account type** and choose student.
3. Confirm the change.

After switching, you lose access to section management, roster view, progress tracking, and assignment controls. Any remaining demo sections are deleted. This cannot be undone.
