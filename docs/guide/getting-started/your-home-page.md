The teacher home page is the starting point for managing classes, assignments, and student activity. Select **My Dashboard** in the top navigation bar to reach it from anywhere in CodeAI.

![The teacher home page with a section card showing an assigned course and enrolled students, the navigation bar, and the New class section button](images/your-home-page-home.png)

## Section cards

Each section you teach appears as a card showing the section name, the assigned course (if any), and a summary of recent student activity. Select a card to open that section's roster, progress, or settings.

![A section card showing the section name, assigned course, and section code](images/your-home-page-section-cards.png)

## Announcements

Above the section cards, CodeAI may display announcements about new courses, features, or events. These dismiss when you close them.

## Quick actions

Below the section cards, links let you create a new section, browse the curriculum catalog, or explore professional learning.

![The New class section button on the teacher home page](images/your-home-page-new-class-section.png)
