title: Reset your password

---

If you forgot your password, you can get a reset link sent to the email address on your account.

1. Go to [studio.code.org/users/password/new](https://studio.code.org/users/password/new).
2. Enter the email address you used when you created your account.
3. Select **Submit**.

Check your email for a link from CodeAI. Open the link and choose a new password.

## Troubleshooting

### You do not receive the email

Check your spam or junk folder. Make sure you entered the same email address you signed up with.

### The reset page shows an error

The page may fail to load if reCAPTCHA cannot connect. Reload the page and try again. If the error continues, contact [support@code.org](mailto:support@code.org).

### You are already signed in as someone else

The reset link may redirect you to the home page. Sign out first, then visit the reset page again.

## If you signed up with Google, Microsoft, Clever, or ClassLink

You do not have a CodeAI password. Go to the [sign-in page](https://studio.code.org/users/sign_in) and select the matching **Continue with...** button.

## If your teacher created your account

Accounts that use a picture or word password do not have an email for password reset. Ask your teacher to reset your password from the section management page.
