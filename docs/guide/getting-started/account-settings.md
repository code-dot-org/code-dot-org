All account settings are on one page. Select your name in the top-right corner, then select **Settings**.

![The account settings page showing the Account information form with display name, username, email, and password fields](images/manage-your-settings-settings-page.png)

## Display name

Your display name appears on rosters, shared projects, and in co-teacher views. Type a new name under **Display name** and select **Save**.

![The Account information form showing Display name, Username, Email address, and Password fields with a Save button](images/manage-your-settings-account-information.png)

**Teacher-managed student accounts:** the teacher manages the display name. Ask the teacher to change it, or [upgrade to a personal account](/guide/getting-started/upgrade-to-a-personal-account/) to manage it yourself.

## Email address

Your email address is used for sign-in and password resets. Enter a new address under **Email** and select **Save**. CodeAI sends a verification email to the new address; password resets go to the old address until you verify the new one.

**Teacher-managed student accounts** without an email address: see [Upgrade to a personal account](/guide/getting-started/upgrade-to-a-personal-account/).

## Password

Enter your current password, then enter and confirm a new one, then select **Save**. If you forgot your current password, [reset it by email](/guide/getting-started/reset-your-password/).

If you signed up with Google, Microsoft, Clever, or ClassLink, you may not have a CodeAI password. You can add one from **Settings** so you have a second way to sign in.

## School (teacher accounts)

Your school association connects you with professional learning opportunities and local resources. Search for and select your school under **School**, then select **Save**.

![The School information section showing a country selector and Update school information button](images/manage-your-settings-school-info.png)

## Email preferences

CodeAI sends emails about account activity, courses, and events. Select or clear the types you want in the email preferences section. You can also select **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any CodeAI email.

Teacher email types include:

- **Account emails** -- password reset, email verification, account deletion.
- **Student activity** -- a student leaves your section.
- **Announcements** -- new courses, features, and Hour of Code campaigns.
- **Inactivity warning** -- advance notice before CodeAI deletes a long-inactive account.

## Sign-in methods

You can link a Google, Microsoft, Facebook, Clever, or ClassLink account as an additional sign-in method, or remove one you no longer use.

![The sign-in methods section listing linked providers with options to connect or disconnect each](images/manage-your-settings-sign-in-methods.png)

- **Link a provider:** in the sign-in methods section, select the provider, then complete authorization on that provider's page.
- **Remove a provider:** select **Disconnect** next to the provider. You must keep at least one working sign-in method; if the provider is your only one, add an email and password first.

Students under a certain age in a covered US state may not be able to link a new sign-in method until a parent or guardian grants permission. See [Parent permission](/guide/privacy/parent-permission/).

## Account type

Under **Account Type**, the dropdown shows whether you are a student or teacher. To switch, see [Switch account type](/guide/getting-started/switch-account-type/).

![The Account Type section with a dropdown showing the current account type and a Change button](images/manage-your-settings-account-type.png)

## Delete account

At the bottom of the page, **Delete Account** permanently removes your account. See [Delete your account](/guide/getting-started/delete-your-account/).

![The Delete Account section at the bottom of the settings page](images/manage-your-settings-delete-account.png)
