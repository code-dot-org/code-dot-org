title: Delete your account
description: Permanently delete your CodeAI account and understand what happens to your data.

---

Deleting your account removes your profile, progress, and projects. This cannot be undone.

## Before you delete

- Download or save any projects you want to keep.
- **Teacher accounts:** students enrolled in your sections keep their own accounts and personal progress, but they lose the teacher relationship and section-specific data may become inaccessible.
- **Student accounts:** if you are enrolled in a section, your teacher loses access to your progress in that section.

## Delete your account

1. Select your name in the top-right corner, then select **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and select **Delete Account**.
3. Confirm the deletion. Teacher accounts must type the confirmation text.

Your account is removed immediately.

## If you cannot delete your account

Students with teacher-managed accounts (picture or word password, no personal email) cannot delete their own account. Ask your teacher to remove you from the section or to delete the account.
