---
title: Upgrade to a personal account
description: Add your own email and password to a teacher-created account so you can sign in independently.
type: task
---

If your teacher created your account, it uses a picture or word password tied to a section code. Adding your own email address and password lets you sign in on any device without the section code. Your section enrollment and progress stay the same.

1. Sign in with your section code or current method.
2. Follow the on-screen prompt to add an email address and password, or go to **Settings** and add them there.

After upgrading, you can sign in at [studio.code.org/users/sign_in](https://studio.code.org/users/sign_in) with your email and password.

## Under-13 accounts in covered US states

A parent or guardian must grant permission before you can complete the upgrade. See [Parent permission](/guide/privacy/parent-permission/).
