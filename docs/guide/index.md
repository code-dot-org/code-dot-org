This guide covers everything you need to use CodeAI in your classroom, from creating accounts to tracking student progress.

- [Getting started](/guide/getting-started/) -- create an account, sign in, and manage your settings.
- [Sections and rosters](/guide/sections/) -- set up a class, choose login types, and manage students.
- [Curriculum](/guide/curriculum/) -- find, assign, and teach courses.
- [Labs](/guide/labs/) -- workspace reference for each lab, plus working on levels and using blocks.
- [Projects and sharing](/guide/projects/) -- manage, share, and publish student projects.
- [Progress and feedback](/guide/progress/) -- track progress, give feedback, and view grades.
- [AI](/guide/ai/) -- AI features in CodeAI and how to control them.
- [Professional learning](/guide/professional-learning/) -- workshops, self-paced courses, and verified teacher status.
- [Integrations and school data](/guide/integrations/) -- connect an LMS, roster from Clever or Google Classroom, and view reports.
- [Privacy and parents](/guide/privacy/) -- parent permission, privacy terms, and what teachers can see.
