CodeAI has no district dashboard. There is no way for district staff to sign in and see aggregated progress, roster data, or usage statistics across schools. This page describes how to get district data today and states what is not available.

## How to get district data

Contact one of the following:

- **Your regional partner.** CodeAI's [regional partners](https://code.org/educate/regional-partner) work directly with districts and can share usage data and professional-learning reports for schools in their region.
- **CodeAI support.** For districts without a regional partner, or for specific data requests, contact [support.code.org](https://support.code.org).

Individual teachers see their own students' progress on the teacher dashboard. If you also teach a section, you have the same progress views as any teacher.

## What is not available

- **No district administrator role.** CodeAI accounts are either student or teacher. Signing in through Clever as a district administrator creates a standard teacher account with no additional permissions.
- **No cross-school view.** A teacher sees only their own sections. There is no way to view another teacher's sections or students, even within the same school.
- **No automated reporting.** CodeAI does not send scheduled reports or data exports to district staff.
