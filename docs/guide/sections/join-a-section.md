A six-letter section code connects you to your teacher's class on CodeAI.

## Prerequisites

- A CodeAI account. If you do not have one, you can create one during the join process.

## Join with a code

1. Go to [code.org/join](https://code.org/join).
2. Type the six-letter section code your teacher gave you.
3. Select **Go**.

If you are not signed in, sign in or create an account first, then try again. After you join, the class and any assigned courses appear on your home page.

## Leave a section

Leaving a section removes it from your home page. Your work and projects stay on your account.

1. On your home page, find the section under your courses.
2. Select **Leave section** next to the section name.

Your teacher can see that you left.

## Troubleshooting

### "Could not find a section with code..."

Check the code your teacher gave you. Codes are six letters and not case-sensitive. If the code is correct and the error persists, ask your teacher to confirm the code from their section settings.

### Already in this section

CodeAI tells you if you are already enrolled. You do not need to join again.
