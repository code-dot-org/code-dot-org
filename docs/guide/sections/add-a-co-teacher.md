A co-teacher can manage the roster, view student work, and track progress alongside you. Each section supports up to five co-teachers.

## Prerequisites

- You are the primary teacher (the one who created the section).
- The co-teacher has a CodeAI teacher account.

## Invite a co-teacher

1. Open your section and select **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Co-teachers**, select **Add Co-Teachers**.

   ![The Add Co-Teachers section on the section settings page, showing the heading and info icon](images/add-a-co-teacher-co-teacher-settings.png)

3. Enter the email address on the co-teacher's CodeAI account.
4. Select **Add**.

The co-teacher sees the invitation on their teacher home page and can accept or decline it.

## Remove a co-teacher

1. Open the section's **Settings**.
2. Under **Co-teachers**, select **Remove** next to the co-teacher.

The primary teacher cannot be removed.

## What co-teachers can do

Co-teachers have the same access as the primary teacher: manage the roster, view progress, give feedback, and see student work. They cannot remove the primary teacher or delete the section.

If the section was imported from an LMS, co-teacher changes must be made in the LMS and synced.
