When you create a section, you choose how students sign in. The login type cannot be changed after the section is created.

![The login type cards: Picture password, Secret words, and Personal logins.](images/choose-a-login-type-login-type-picker.png)

| Login type | Recommended ages | How students sign in |
|---|---|---|
| **Picture password** | 4--8 | Click a sequence of pictures. No typing or email needed. |
| **Secret words** | 9--12 | Type a pair of assigned secret words. No email needed. |
| **Personal logins** | 13+ | Create their own account with email and password. |
| **Google Classroom** | Any | Sign in with their Google accounts. Roster syncs automatically. |
| **Clever** | Any | Sign in through Clever. Roster syncs automatically. |

**Picture password** and **Secret words** sections let you print sign-in cards for each student. **Google Classroom** and **Clever** sections manage the class list in the external service, not in CodeAI.

If you need to change the login type later, [create a new section](/guide/sections/create-a-section/) with the login type you want and [move your students](/guide/sections/managing-your-roster/).
