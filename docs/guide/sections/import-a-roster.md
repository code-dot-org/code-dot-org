If your class list already exists in Google Classroom, Clever, or a learning management system, you can import it when creating a section. The imported roster stays in sync with the external service.

## Import from Google Classroom

1. On your teacher home page, select **New class section**.
2. Choose **Google Classroom** as the login type.
3. Sign in with your Google account and authorize CodeAI.
4. Select the Google Classroom class to import.
5. Select **Save**.

Students sign in with their Google accounts. To update the roster, make changes in Google Classroom and sync.

## Import from Clever

1. On your teacher home page, select **New class section**.
2. Choose **Clever** as the login type.
3. Sign in with your Clever account.
4. Select the Clever class to import.

Students sign in through Clever. Manage the roster in Clever and sync to pull updates.

## Import from an LMS

LMS integration (Canvas, Schoology, and others) uses LTI. Your school or district administrator sets it up. Once configured:

1. In your LMS, launch a CodeAI assignment.
2. CodeAI creates a section and syncs the roster automatically.
3. To re-sync, select **Sync students & sections** on the section's **Login Info** page.

For LMS setup details, see the [LMS integration guide](/guide/integrations/connect-your-lms/).

## Limitations

You cannot manually add or remove students in an imported section. Manage the roster in the external service and sync. Co-teacher changes in LMS sections must also be made in the LMS.
