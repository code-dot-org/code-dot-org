The **Roster** tab on the teacher dashboard is where you manage the students in a section.

![The Roster tab showing four enrolled students with display name, age, and state columns](images/managing-your-roster-roster.png)

## Prerequisites

- A section you own or co-teach. See [Create a section](/guide/sections/create-a-section/).

## Add students

For sections with personal login, picture password, or secret words:

1. On the **Roster** tab, select **Add students**.
2. Enter each student's display name, one per row.
3. Select **Save**.

CodeAI creates accounts for each student. For picture password and secret words sections, it assigns a secret you can print from the **Login Info** page.

If your section uses Google Classroom or Clever, add students in the external service and sync.

## Edit a student's name

![A single student row on the roster showing the student's display name and the actions column](images/managing-your-roster-roster-row-controls.png)

1. On the **Roster** tab, open the actions menu (three-dot icon) next to the student.
2. Select **Edit**.
3. Change the display name and select **Save**.

## Reset a student's password or secret

### Picture password or secret words sections

On the **Roster** tab, find the student's row. In the secret column, select **Show picture** or **Show words** to see the current secret, then select **Reset** to generate a new one. Print an updated login card from the **Login Info** page.

### Personal login sections

1. On the **Roster** tab, open the actions menu next to the student and select **Edit**.
2. Enter a new password and select **Save**.

## Remove a student

1. On the **Roster** tab, open the actions menu next to the student and select **Remove student**.
2. Confirm the removal.

Removing a student does not delete their account or work. They lose access to the section's assigned course on their home page but keep everything they completed.

## Section capacity

A section holds up to 500 students.

## Print login cards

1. On your teacher home page, select the section.
2. Open the **Login Info** page from the section card.
3. Select **Print**.

Each card shows the student's name, the section code, and sign-in instructions. For picture password and secret words sections, the card includes the assigned secret.

## Print a parent letter

From the section card or section settings, select **Parent letter** and print. The letter explains what data CodeAI collects and how the student signs in. It is especially useful for students under 13.

## Move students between sections

You need to own or co-teach both sections, and both must be student sections.

1. Open the source section and select **Roster** in the sidebar.
2. Select the students you want to move.
3. Select **Move students**.
4. Choose the destination section and confirm.

Students appear on the destination roster immediately and their work moves with them. To keep students in the original section as well, select **Copy students** instead.

## Troubleshooting

### A student says the section code is invalid

- The student may have mistyped a letter. The code is not case-sensitive but every letter matters.
- The code works at [code.org/join](https://code.org/join), not on the sign-in page.
- The section may be archived. Archived sections do not accept new joins.

### A student cannot sign in after joining

- **Picture password or secret words:** print a fresh login card from the **Login Info** page and confirm the student is using the correct secret. [Reset the secret](#picture-password-or-secret-words-sections) if needed.
- **Personal login:** [reset the password from the roster](#personal-login-sections) or have the student use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
- **Google Classroom or Clever:** the student must sign in through their Google or Clever account, not with a section code.
