Archiving removes a section from your dashboard and from students' home pages without deleting any data. You can restore it at any time.

## Prerequisites

- You own or co-teach the section.

## Archive one section

1. On your teacher home page, find the section card.
2. Open the section menu (three-dot icon on the card) and select **Archive**.

   ![The section card with the three-dot menu open, showing options for Section settings, Roster, Login cards, Certificates, and Archive](images/archive-a-section-section-menu.png)

## Archive all sections at once

On your teacher home page, select **Archive all class sections** and confirm. This archives every section you teach, including sections where you are a co-teacher.

## Restore a section

1. On your teacher home page, expand the archived sections area.
2. Find the section and select **Restore Section**.

The section reappears on your dashboard and on students' home pages.

## What archiving does

- Students stay in the section. Their accounts and work are untouched.
- Students stop seeing the section's assigned course on their home page.
- The section code stops accepting new joins.
- You can still view the roster, progress, and assessment data from the archived list.
