After creating a section, you can change its name, grade levels, and join restrictions. The login type cannot be changed.

## Prerequisites

- You own or co-teach the section.

## Edit settings

1. Open your section and select **Settings** in the sidebar.

   ![The section settings form showing the section name, grade selector, and login type fields](images/edit-section-settings-settings-form.png)

2. Change what you need:
   - **Section name** -- what you and your students see.
   - **Grade** -- the grade levels for this section.
   - **Restrict section** -- when enabled, students cannot join using the section code. You can still add students manually or through roster sync.
3. Select **Save**.

## Change the login type

The login type is permanent. Create a new section with the login type you want and [move your students](/guide/sections/managing-your-roster/). See [Choose a login type](/guide/sections/choose-a-login-type/).
