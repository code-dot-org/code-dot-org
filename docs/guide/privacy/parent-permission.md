In some US states, students under a certain age who create their own account must have a parent or guardian grant permission before the account is fully active. This is required by children's privacy laws.

## Who needs parent permission

All of these must apply:

- You are in a US state with a child account policy (such as Colorado).
- You are under the age set by that state's policy.
- You created a personal account with your own email address.

Students whose teacher created their account through a section are not affected.

## What happens after you sign up

1. CodeAI tells you that parent permission is required.
2. You enter your parent or guardian's email address.
3. CodeAI sends them an email with a link to grant permission.
4. Until they respond, your account is in a grace period. You can still sign in and work on courses, but some features may be limited.
5. If your parent or guardian grants permission, your account becomes fully active.
6. If nobody responds by the deadline shown on the permission page, your account is locked and eventually deleted.

## If you entered the wrong parent email

Update the email address on the permission page and send a new request.
