---

Your child created a CodeAI account, and your state's children's privacy laws require your permission before the account is fully active.

## Grant permission

1. Open the link in the email. You do not need a CodeAI account.
2. Review the information about what data CodeAI collects and how it is used.
3. Select the option to grant permission.

Your child's account becomes fully active immediately.

## If you do not want to grant permission

Do nothing. If no one responds by the deadline shown in the email (typically 7 to 10 days), the account is locked and eventually deleted. Your child can create a new account later and request permission again.

## What your child can do while waiting

During the waiting period, your child can sign in and use CodeAI with limited features.

## What data CodeAI collects

CodeAI collects limited data to provide the service:

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Display name | Used to greet your child; a first name is enough. |
| Username | Chosen during sign-up. |
| Password | Stored encrypted. |
| Email address | Hashed; CodeAI cannot use it to contact your child. |
| Age | Not birthday. |
| Gender | Optional; used only for aggregate measurement. |

CodeAI does not sell personal information and does not display advertising. The full privacy policy, including the children's privacy notice, is at [code.org/privacy](https://code.org/privacy).
