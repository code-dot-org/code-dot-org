CodeAI may ask you to review and accept updated terms when you sign in. You must accept the current terms to continue using your account.

- **Terms of Service** -- the rules for using CodeAI. [code.org/tos](https://code.org/tos)
- **Privacy Policy** -- how CodeAI collects, uses, and protects your data. [code.org/privacy](https://code.org/privacy)

## Cookie consent

When you first visit CodeAI, a banner may ask whether you accept cookies and tracking. Your choice affects analytics and advertising cookies, not the cookies needed for sign-in.

## EU data transfer

If you are in a European country, you may be asked to agree to a data transfer agreement the first time you sign in. This covers the transfer of your data to servers in the United States.
