---
title: What teachers can see
description: What information about your child is visible to their teacher in CodeAI.
type: reference
---

When your child is enrolled in a teacher's section on CodeAI, the teacher can see:

- Your child's display name.
- Their progress on assigned courses and activities.
- Their responses to assessments.
- Whether their account is waiting for parent permission (in covered US states).

## What teachers cannot see

- Your child's email address (unless the teacher created the account).
- Your email address.
- Your child's password.
- Projects your child has not shared with the section.

## If your child leaves a section

When your child is removed from a section or leaves on their own, the teacher can no longer see their progress in that section.
