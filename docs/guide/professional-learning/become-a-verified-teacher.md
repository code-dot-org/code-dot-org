Verified teachers can see answer keys, exemplar code, and other instructor-only resources in CodeAI courses. Verification is automatic in most cases.

## How you become verified

You are automatically verified when any of the following happens:

- You enroll in a workshop for CS Discoveries, CS Principles, CS A, or Build Your Own.
- You enroll in a self-paced PLC course.
- You sign in through a school-managed provider (Clever, ClassLink, or LTI).
- You sync a Google Classroom section and your Google account uses a school email domain (not @gmail.com, @googlemail.com, or @google.com).
- You submit the international workshop attendance form at `/pd/international_workshop`.

If none of these paths apply, contact [CodeAI support](https://support.code.org) to request manual verification.

## Apply to the Professional Learning Program

For CS Discoveries, CS Principles, and CS A, CodeAI runs a year-long Professional Learning Program. Applications are submitted on [code.org/apply](https://code.org/apply). When your application is accepted and you are enrolled in a workshop, verification is granted automatically.

## What verification unlocks

Verified status gives you access to locked instructor content across all courses you can teach. It does not change which courses you can see or assign. See [Who can see this course](/guide/curriculum/who-can-see-this-course/).

## Check your verification status

If answer keys and exemplar code appear on levels that include them, you are verified. If they do not, try one of the paths above.

Teachers who lead workshops (facilitators) or who have been given other instructor-level permissions by CodeAI staff can see locked content without separate verification.
