The workshop dashboard lets facilitators, organizers, and regional partners manage professional learning workshops.

![The workshop dashboard showing the Your Workshops list with action buttons and workshop details](images/manage-workshops-workshop-dashboard.png)

## Prerequisites

- You have a facilitator, workshop organizer, program manager, or workshop admin permission.

## Access the workshop dashboard

1. Go to your [Professional Learning](https://studio.code.org/my-professional-learning) page.
2. Select the tab that matches your role: **Facilitator Center**, **Workshop Organizer**, or **Regional Partner Center**.
3. Select **View workshop dashboard** to open the full dashboard at `/pd/workshop_dashboard`.

What you see depends on your permission:

| Permission | Scope |
|---|---|
| Workshop admin | All workshops and regional partners. |
| Workshop organizer or program manager | Workshops and applications for their regional partner. |
| Facilitator | Workshops where they are assigned. |

## Create and manage workshops

Organizers and admins can create workshops, add sessions, assign facilitators, set capacity, and manage enrollment lists from the dashboard.

## Record attendance

Facilitators and organizers mark participants present or absent for each session from the attendance view.

## Reports

The dashboard provides a workshop summary report, a per-teacher attendance report, and aggregated survey reports. Reports are available to workshop admins, organizers, and program managers.

## Assign facilitators to courses

Workshop admins can assign which courses a facilitator is allowed to lead from `/pd/workshop_user_management/facilitator_courses`.
