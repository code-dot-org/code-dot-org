CodeAI works with regional partner organizations across the United States to deliver professional learning locally.

## Send a message

1. Go to [Contact your local Regional Partner](https://studio.code.org/professional-learning/contact-regional-partner).
2. Fill in your **Name**, **Email**, and **School ZIP Code**.
3. Select the grade levels you teach (**K-5**, **6-8**, **9-12**).
4. Optionally enter your role.
5. Write your question in the **Questions or notes for your local Regional Partner** field.
6. Select **Send**.

Your message is sent to the regional partner matched to your ZIP code.

If no regional partner is matched (not every ZIP code has one), contact [CodeAI support](https://support.code.org) directly.
