CodeAI workshops are multi-day, facilitator-led sessions where you explore a curriculum hands-on and connect with other educators.

## Find workshops near you

1. Go to [Professional Learning Workshops](https://studio.code.org/professional-learning/workshops).
2. Enter your school ZIP code and select **Submit**.

The page shows your regional partner and any upcoming local workshops. National workshops, if available, appear at the top regardless of ZIP code.

If no workshops appear, they may not yet be scheduled in your area. Check back later or [contact your regional partner](/guide/professional-learning/contact-your-regional-partner/).

## Enroll in a workshop

Select the enrollment link on the workshop you want to attend. You need a CodeAI teacher account. After enrolling:

- You receive a confirmation email with workshop details.
- The workshop appears on your [Professional Learning](https://studio.code.org/my-professional-learning) page.
- You can cancel from the enrollment confirmation page using the cancellation link in the email.
