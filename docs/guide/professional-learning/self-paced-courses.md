Self-paced professional learning courses let you prepare to teach a CodeAI curriculum on your own schedule. Each course includes readings, videos, interactive activities, and reflections.

## Browse available courses

1. Go to [Explore self-paced professional learning](https://studio.code.org/professional-learning/courses).
2. Select **Start** on any course to begin.

You need a CodeAI teacher account. Your progress is saved automatically.

Courses cover CS Fundamentals (K-5), CS Discoveries, CS Principles, CS A, AI Foundations, AI 101, and Equity in Computer Science, among others. Some courses are designed specifically for facilitators and require the facilitator permission.

## Track your progress

Once started, the course appears on your [Professional Learning](https://studio.code.org/my-professional-learning) page with a **Continue course** link and your completion status.
