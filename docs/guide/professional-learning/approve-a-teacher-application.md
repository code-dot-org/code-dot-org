When a teacher applies to CodeAI's Professional Learning Program and names you as their school administrator, the program may require your approval before the application advances. This page covers how the approval works and what it commits you to.

No CodeAI account is required. The process happens entirely through an emailed link.

## How approval works

Teachers apply at [code.org/apply](https://code.org/apply). When administrator approval is required, CodeAI or your regional partner sends you an email containing a link to a short form. The email comes from a `@code.org` address or from the regional partner organization serving your area.

The form asks for your name, title, basic school information, and whether you approve of the teacher's participation. After you submit the form, the teacher's application advances to review.

Approving does not commit your school to any cost. The Professional Learning Program may carry a fee, but payment is handled separately from approval, and scholarships may be available. See [code.org/educate/professional-learning/program-information](https://code.org/educate/professional-learning/program-information) for pricing.

## Troubleshooting

### You did not receive the email

Ask the teacher to confirm they entered your correct email address in their application at [code.org/apply](https://code.org/apply). Check your spam or junk folder.

### You are unsure the email is legitimate

The approval link points to a form on `code.org`. If you are unsure, do not click it. Go directly to [code.org/educate/professional-learning](https://code.org/educate/professional-learning) for program information, or [contact CodeAI support](https://support.code.org) to verify.
