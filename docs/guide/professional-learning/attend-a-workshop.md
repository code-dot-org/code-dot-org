This page covers what happens during and after a workshop: recording attendance, completing surveys, and downloading your certificate.

## Record your attendance

Your facilitator may give you a session attendance code.

1. Go to the attendance URL your facilitator provides (looks like `/pd/attend/<session-code>`).
2. Sign in if prompted or confirm your enrollment.

Your attendance is recorded for that session. If you are not yet enrolled, the attendance page lets you join and records attendance in one step.

## Complete the survey

After the workshop ends, you may receive a feedback survey link by email. Surveys also appear on your [Professional Learning](https://studio.code.org/my-professional-learning) page. Types include daily surveys (one per day during multi-day workshops), pre-workshop surveys, and post-workshop surveys.

## Download your certificate

After your attendance is recorded and the workshop has ended:

1. Go to your [Professional Learning](https://studio.code.org/my-professional-learning) page.
2. Find the workshop in your history.
3. Select the certificate link to generate and download a PDF.

## Troubleshooting

### Attendance was not recorded

Contact your facilitator or [your regional partner](/guide/professional-learning/contact-your-regional-partner/). They can update attendance records from the workshop dashboard.
