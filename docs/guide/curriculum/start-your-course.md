---

When you sign in, each course your teacher assigned appears as a card on your home page.

## Find your assigned course

1. Sign in to CodeAI.
2. Select the card for the course you want to start.

CodeAI takes you to the first lesson if you have not started the course, or to your most recent unfinished lesson if you have.

## Multiple sections

If you belong to more than one section, each section's assigned course appears on your home page. Select the one you want to work on.

## If no course appears

Your teacher may not have assigned one yet. Ask your teacher which course to work on.
