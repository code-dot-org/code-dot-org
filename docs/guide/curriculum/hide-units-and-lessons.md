title: Hide units and lessons
description: Control which parts of a course your students can see and work on.
type: task
---

You can hide lessons or entire units so students cannot see or work on them. Hidden content disappears from students' progress views and course navigation. Student progress on hidden content is preserved and reappears when you make it visible again.

## Prerequisites

- A section with an assigned course. See [Assign a course](/guide/curriculum/assign-a-course/).

## Hide or show a lesson

1. Open your section and navigate to the course view.

   ![The course view showing lessons with visibility toggles next to each lesson name](images/hide-units-and-lessons-lesson-visibility.png)

2. Select the visibility toggle next to the lesson.

## Hide or show an entire unit

Select the visibility toggle next to the unit name. All lessons in the unit are hidden or shown together.

Visibility is per-section: hiding content in one section does not affect other sections. Any teacher or co-teacher for the section can change visibility.
