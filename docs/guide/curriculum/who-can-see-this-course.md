Whether a course is visible depends on its published state.

## Courses in the curriculum catalog

Any course in the [curriculum catalog](/guide/curriculum/find-a-course/) is accessible to everyone -- students, teachers, and visitors who are not signed in. The catalog shows courses in the **preview** or **stable** state that are marked assignable and have a student audience.

## Courses not in the catalog

A course can exist but not appear in the catalog:

| Published state | Who can access |
|---|---|
| **In development** | CodeAI curriculum authors only. |
| **Pilot** | Curriculum authors, teachers enrolled in the pilot, and their students. |
| **Beta** | Anyone with a direct URL. Not listed in the catalog. Still in progress. |
| **Sunsetting** or **Deprecated** | Anyone with a direct URL. No longer updated. |

## Teacher verification and visibility

Being a verified teacher does not change which courses you can see. Verification unlocks answer keys, exemplar code, and instructor-only resources within courses you already have access to.

## Which courses can be assigned

A course can be assigned to a section when it is marked assignable, its audience matches the section, and you have permission to teach it. Most K-12 courses allow any teacher. Some professional-learning courses require specific permissions.

## Course versions

When a course has more than one version year, you see the latest stable version by default. Students see only the version they are assigned to or have progress in.
