An Hour of Code is a one-hour introduction to computer science. No account is needed.

1. Go to the activity link your teacher or event organizer shared, or visit [studio.code.org/hoc/1](https://studio.code.org/hoc/1) for the classic tutorial.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions. Each puzzle teaches a new concept.
3. When you finish, a congratulations page appears with an option to print a certificate.

Your progress is saved in your browser while you work. If you close the tab and come back without signing in, you may need to start over.

## Get a certificate when you finish

On the congratulations page, enter your name to receive a certificate you can print or share. See [Get your certificate](/guide/curriculum/certificates/).

## Create an account afterward

Creating a free CodeAI account saves your work and lets you keep learning. It is optional.
