Pair programming lets two students work on the same level together. Both accounts record progress for the work done during the session.

**Applies to:** sections with the Picture or Word login type, where the teacher has allowed pairing.

## Start a paired session

1. Sign in with your section code.
2. After you select your name, check the **Pair Programming** box.
3. Follow the on-screen pairing dialog to connect with your partner.

Only levels you work on while the pairing is active count as shared. Levels completed before pairing stay on your own account.

## End the session

End the pairing session through the on-screen controls. After that, each of you works independently.
