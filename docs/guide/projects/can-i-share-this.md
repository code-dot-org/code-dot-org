Whether you can share a project depends on the project type, your age, and your teacher's settings.

## Sharing by lab type

Projects in most labs (Artist, Dance, Sprite Lab, Music Lab, Python Lab, and many others) can be shared by any student.

**App Lab and Game Lab:** if you are 13 or older, sharing is on unless your teacher turned it off. If you are under 13, sharing is off unless your teacher turned it on. When sharing is not available, the share button is disabled and you see "Sharing is disabled."

**Web Lab, Algebra, and Minecraft Code Builder:** these project types cannot be published to the public gallery.

## Uploaded images

If you upload your own image in Sprite Lab, the project enters restricted share mode: you can share it with a link, but you cannot publish it to the public gallery and others cannot remix it. To undo this, use [Version History](/guide/projects/managing-your-projects/) to restore a version before the upload.

## Under-13 accounts

If you are under 13, sharing is disabled by default. Your teacher can enable it in their section settings. When sharing is enabled, you see a reminder: "Ask your teacher before sharing. Only share with others in your school."

## Personal information warnings

When you share a project, CodeAI checks your code for text that looks like a name, email, or phone number. If it finds something, it shows a warning and asks you to remove the personal information before sharing.
