The teacher dashboard lets you view what students have built outside assigned lessons and control whether they can share projects.

## Prerequisites

- A section with at least one enrolled student.

## View student projects

1. On the teacher dashboard, select a section.
2. Select the **Student Projects** tab.

The list shows each student's standalone projects with the project name, lab type, and last-edited date. Select any project to open it in view mode. You cannot edit, delete, or restore earlier versions of a student's project.

## Manage sharing for your section

Sharing controls whether students can send project links and publish to the public gallery.

Most project types (Artist, Dance, Sprite Lab, Music Lab, and others) are always shareable regardless of age or settings.

**App Lab and Game Lab** are different because they allow free-form text and uploaded content:

- Students 13 or older can share unless you have disabled sharing for the section.
- Students under 13 cannot share unless you have enabled sharing for the section.

When you create a section, sharing follows the default for the age group.

### Change the sharing setting

1. On the teacher dashboard, select the section.
2. Select **Settings** in the sidebar.
3. Toggle the sharing setting and select **Save**.

The change applies to all students in that section. If a student belongs to multiple sections, any section that disables sharing disables it for that student across App Lab and Game Lab.

When sharing is disabled, students see "Sharing is disabled" in place of the share dialog for App Lab and Game Lab projects. Under-13 students who can share see a reminder to ask their teacher first.

## Share a library with your section

**Applies to:** App Lab.

If a student or teacher has published a useful App Lab code library, you can share it with an entire section. Students in that section can then import the library without searching for it.

1. On the teacher dashboard, select the section.
2. In the section's library settings, add the library by its ID or URL.
