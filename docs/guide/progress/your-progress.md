Your progress saves automatically every time you run or submit your work. The progress view shows your status across all levels in a course.

![The progress view for an assigned course, showing the unit overview with chapter headings, expanded lesson content, and green filled circles for completed levels](images/your-progress-progress-view.png)

## Progress view

1. Sign in to CodeAI.
2. On your home page, select your assigned course.

The unit overview shows a colored circle for each level.

![Progress bubbles for a lesson showing green filled circles numbered 1 through 5, grouped under Exploration and Skill Building activity labels](images/your-progress-status-indicators.png)

| Circle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Filled | You finished this level. |
| Partially filled | You started but did not finish. |
| Empty | You have not opened this level. |
| Different color | You submitted this level for review. |

The exact colors depend on the theme, but the fill pattern indicates the status.

## Teacher feedback

When your teacher leaves feedback on your work, the progress circle for that level shows a small indicator. Open the level to read the comment. If your teacher evaluated your work with a rubric, the rubric scores appear alongside any comments.

If your teacher marked your work as "keep working," revise and submit again.

## Code review

**Applies to:** sections with code review groups.

If your teacher set up code review groups, you can read group members' code and leave comments.

1. Open the code review area (your teacher tells you which level).
2. Select a classmate's name to open their project.
3. Read their code and type a comment.
4. Submit your comment.

Your classmate sees your comment the next time they open their project.
