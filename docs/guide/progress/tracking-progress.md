The teacher dashboard shows which lessons each student has started, finished, or submitted, and gives you tools to read text responses, review assessments, and lock lessons for timed work.

![The teacher dashboard Progress tab showing a progress grid with four students and level completion indicators across lessons](images/tracking-progress-progress.png)

## Prerequisites

- A section with an [assigned course](/guide/curriculum/assign-a-course/) and at least one enrolled student.

## Progress grid

On the teacher dashboard, select your section and then **Progress** in the sidebar. If the course has multiple units, use the unit selector at the top to switch between them. Select a lesson column header to expand it and see individual levels.

![The teacher dashboard sidebar showing the section selector and navigation tabs: Course, Lesson Materials, Calendar, Progress, Assessments, Student Projects, Stats, Text Responses, Student Snapshot, Roster, Settings, and AI Settings](images/tracking-progress-sidebar-tabs.png)

### Progress icons

Each cell shows the student's status:

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not started | The student has not opened this lesson. |
| In progress | Started but not finished. |
| Completed | Every level in the lesson is finished. |
| Submitted | The student submitted their work. |
| Needs feedback | Submitted and waiting for your feedback. |
| Keep working | You asked the student to revise. |

Select **Icon Key** at the top of the progress table to see the exact shapes and colors.

### Student details

Select a student's row to see **Last Updated** and **Time Spent** per lesson. Select a level cell to open that student's work in a new tab with the teacher panel visible. From there you can [give feedback or grade with a rubric](/guide/progress/giving-feedback-and-grading/).

## Text responses

The **Text Responses** tab collects every free-response answer so you can read them without opening each student's work individually.

1. Select **Text Responses** in the sidebar.
2. Use the unit and lesson selectors to narrow to the level you want.

![The Text Responses tab showing a student's name, the lesson and level, the question prompt, and the student's written answer](images/tracking-progress-text-responses.png)

Each entry shows the student's name and what they wrote.

## Assessments

The **Assessments** tab shows results for multi-question assessments and anonymous surveys.

![The Assessments tab with a unit selector, an assessment selector, and a Download CSV of Feedback button](images/tracking-progress-assessments.png)

1. Select **Assessments** in the sidebar.
2. Use the dropdown to choose the assessment.

The results table shows each student's answers, marking which are correct. You can enter a score for each student; scores save automatically and are visible to you and your co-teachers.

### Survey results

Anonymous surveys show aggregate responses; individual answers are not attributed. Students under 13 are excluded from survey data for privacy.

### Download results

Select the download option on the **Assessments** tab to export assessment and feedback data for offline review.

## Lesson locking

**Applies to:** courses with lockable assessments.

Lockable lessons start locked. Students see a lock icon and cannot open them until you unlock.

1. Open your section's course view.
2. Find the lockable lesson and select the lock icon to unlock it.

After the assessment window, select the lock icon again. You can choose whether students can still view their answers (read-only) or are fully locked out. Locking does not erase student work.

## Troubleshooting

### A student's progress does not update

Progress updates when the student completes a level while signed in. If a cell stays blank:

- Confirm the student is signed in to the correct account (not a second account they created).
- Confirm the student joined the correct section. Their section code is on the **Login Info** page.
- Work done while signed out is not linked to their account.

### A course is missing from the catalog

The course catalog is filtered by the grade levels set on your section. Change the grade range in [section settings](/guide/sections/edit-section-settings/) and the catalog updates.
