Some levels ask you to answer questions or submit work instead of writing code.

## Multiple-choice and matching questions

1. Read the question and select your answer.
2. If the level has more than one page, select **Next** to continue.
3. On the last page, select **Submit**.

After you submit, you cannot change your answers unless your teacher unlocks the assessment.

## Free-response questions

Type your answer in the text box and select **Submit**. Your teacher can read your response in their dashboard.

## Reflections

Some lessons end with a reflection prompt. Type your thoughts and select **Submit**. Reflections are not graded, but your teacher can see them.

## AI-evaluated practice problems

**Applies to:** courses with AI-evaluated questions.

Some courses offer practice problems evaluated by AI. Answer the question and submit. You see feedback telling you whether your answer is correct. Challenges are longer: you build or write a response, submit it for AI evaluation, and receive feedback. Your teacher and classmates in your section may be able to see your final submission.

## Project submissions

If a lesson asks you to build something and hand it in, select **Submit** on the project page. Submitting locks the project so your teacher can review it. Your teacher can return it if you need to make changes.
