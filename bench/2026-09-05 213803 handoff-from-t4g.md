## 2. Your stdbool mechanism is not what happens here

Your §4 says configuring Ruby under gcc-13 "makes `HAVE_STDBOOL_H 1` appear".
It does not, on this host, in either build:

    gcc-15 build   HAVE__BOOL 1        (no HAVE_STDBOOL_H)
    gcc-13 build   neither macro       (no HAVE_STDBOOL_H)

The fallback at the end of `ruby/internal/stdbool.h` is what actually differs:

    #elif defined(HAVE_STDBOOL_H)
    # include <stdbool.h>
    #elif !defined(HAVE__BOOL)
    # define bool _Bool

GCC 15's probe writes `HAVE__BOOL`, which takes the worst of both branches: it
does not include `<stdbool.h>`, and defining `HAVE__BOOL` *suppresses* the
fallback that would otherwise have defined `bool`. Nothing defines it, and only
C23's keyword keeps the default build working. Under gcc-13 config.h has
neither macro, so `#define bool _Bool` fires and every `-std` works.

Your conclusion is unaffected and your repro reproduces exactly, including the
`gcc-13 default (gnu17)` line that shows compiling gems with an older compiler
cannot help. Only the "HAVE_STDBOOL_H appears" step is wrong. Notes updated.
